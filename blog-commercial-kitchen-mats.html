Matting for Commercial Kitchens and Restaurants

28 July 2026 Divine Rubber Team 3 min read

ℹDraft for review — the Divine Rubber team will confirm this content and set a publication date before it goes live.

Kitchen floors deal with water, grease and long hours of standing. The right mats make them safer and more comfortable.

What a kitchen mat needs

  • Drainage — open holes let water and food waste fall through.
  • Grip — a textured surface helps keep footing on wet floors.
  • Comfort — cushioning for staff who stand for whole shifts.
  • Easy cleaning — mats that can be lifted, washed and put back quickly.

Our suggestions

The Restaurant Mat (3 ft × 3 ft) covers large areas without connectors and suits restaurants and kitchens. The Universal Pattern hollow mat combines an open-hole top with a studded bottom for comfort and drainage, and can be joined with connectors.

For dry prep areas and counters, an anti-fatigue mat reduces strain on feet, legs and back.

Daily care

Lift and rinse mats at the end of service, clean the floor underneath and let mats dry before putting them back.
