Matting for Commercial Kitchens and Restaurants

16 October 2026 Divine Rubber Team 1 min read

Kitchen floors deal with water, grease and long hours of standing. The right mats make them safer and more comfortable for the people who work there.

What a kitchen mat needs

  • Drainage — open holes let water and food waste fall through.
  • Grip — a textured surface helps keep footing on wet floors.
  • Comfort — cushioning for staff who stand for whole shifts.
  • Easy cleaning — mats that can be lifted, washed and put back quickly.

Mats for each kitchen area

  • Cooking lines and wash-up: the Restaurant Mat (3 ft × 3 ft) covers large areas without connectors and suits restaurants and kitchens.
  • Large or irregular floors: the Universal Pattern hollow mat combines an open-hole top with a studded bottom for comfort and drainage, and joins with connectors.
  • Dry prep areas and counters: an Anti-Fatigue Bubble Mat reduces strain on feet, legs and back.
  • Front of house: an entrance mat stops rain and grit at the door.

Daily care

  • Lift and rinse mats at the end of service.
  • Clean the floor underneath, where food waste collects.
  • Let mats dry before putting them back.

More on wet areas in our drainage mats guide and slip resistance guide.
