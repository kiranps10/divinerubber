Rubber Matting for Factories, Warehouses and Workshops

16 October 2026 Divine Rubber Team 2 min read

Factories, warehouses and workshops are hard on floors — and on the people who work on them. The right rubber matting can cushion heavy-duty areas, ease long shifts at workstations and give walkways a surer footing.

Heavy-duty floor areas

Where a large area needs a durable, cushioned rubber floor, interlocking tiles can be laid — and lifted — one tile at a time:

  • Interlocking Coin Mat — 1 m × 1 m tiles, 22 mm thick, with a coin design on both sides.
  • Interlocking Tile Mat — 50 cm × 50 cm tiles, 20 mm thick, easy to handle and fit around corners and obstacles.

Interlocking edges join the tiles into a continuous floor of any size, and individual tiles can be replaced if one area wears faster than the rest.

Workstations and production lines

Standing on a hard floor for a whole shift is tiring. The Anti-Fatigue Bubble Mat has a raised bubble surface that helps reduce strain on feet, legs and back, and is well suited to workstations, counters and production lines. Read more in our anti-fatigue mats guide.

Corridors and walkways

The Diamond Top Runner is a rubber runner with a diamond-top surface, supplied in rolls that can be cut to length for corridors, walkways and long work areas. Custom colour and logo choices are available.

Wet and messy areas

Where water, oil or debris reach the floor — wash-down zones, canteens and loading areas — open-hole hollow mats let liquid and dirt fall through so the walking surface stays cleaner.

Planning an industrial floor

  • Map the zones: heavy-duty floor, standing positions, walkways and wet areas.
  • Measure each area so tiles and runners can be quantified.
  • Decide where mats need to be lifted for cleaning or access.
  • Ask about thickness and colour options for each zone.
Planning a larger floor? Send us the area sizes and how each zone is used, and we'll suggest a combination of mats.
