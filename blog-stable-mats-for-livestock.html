Why Stable Mats Matter for Cattle and Horse Comfort

8 September 2026 Divine Rubber Team 3 min read

ℹDraft for review — the Divine Rubber team will confirm this content and set a publication date before it goes live.

Bare concrete is hard on animals. Rubber stable mats give cows and horses a cushioned, sure-footed floor and make stables easier to keep clean.

Comfort and protection

Rubber flooring cushions animals' knees, hocks and udders and helps reduce foot problems compared with bare concrete. A textured top — such as the diamond-top surface — gives confident footing.

Hygiene

Divine Rubber stable mats are designed to help keep stables hygienic. A smooth or amebic top surface is easy to clean, and the rubber can be washed down regularly.

Choosing a stable mat

  • Grooved bottom, plain amebic top — grooved underside for stability, smooth top for easy cleaning; 4 ft × 6 ft in 17 mm and 18 mm.
  • Studded bottom, amebic top — studded underside; 4 ft × 6 ft, 18 mm.
  • Diamond Top Stable Mat — heavy 4 ft × 6 ft × 17 mm mat with a diamond-top surface.
  • Interlocking stable mats — join into larger continuous stable floors.
