Coir & Jute

Jute Mats and Rugs for Natural Interiors

16 October 2026 Divine Rubber Team 1 min read

Jute brings a warm, natural texture to a room. Alongside rubber and coir, Divine Rubber offers a range of eco-friendly jute products for homes and quiet commercial spaces.

The jute range

  • Jute loop mats — eco-friendly loop mats, in two designs.
  • Jute Rugs & Carpets — for living spaces and bedrooms.
  • Hand-spun Jute — hand-spun jute matting.
  • Jute Dot Mattings — jute matting with a dot pattern.

The range also includes short-term erosion-control mats for outdoor ground cover.

Where jute works best

Jute is best indoors, in dry areas with light to moderate foot traffic — living rooms, bedrooms, hallways and calm reception areas. It pairs well with natural wood, cotton and linen.

For wet or busy entrances, pair it with a rubber or coir mat at the door, so the jute further inside stays clean and dry.

Choosing a jute mat or rug

  • Size: measure the space and leave a border of floor around the rug.
  • Texture: loop mats and hand-spun jute give a chunkier texture; dot mattings a finer pattern.
  • Traffic: keep jute away from doorways where grit and water come in.

Care

  • Vacuum regularly and keep jute dry.
  • Blot spills straight away rather than rubbing them.

See our mat care guide for more tips, or browse the coir & jute range.
