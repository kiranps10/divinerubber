Our Infrastructure

Facilities, equipment and spaces behind our products.

Infrastructure Overview

The Foundation Behind Our Products

Our infrastructure brings together manufacturing, material processing, storage and dispatch.

Connected spaces at Eara, Neelamperoor, in Kerala's rubber and coir belt, support every stage of the product journey.

Rubber compounds are prepared on the mixing mill from RSS-4 rubber, micro crumb and internationally branded chemicals, then formed into sheets and rolls on the sheeting line. Rubber, coir and jute mats are each finished to their order specification.

Finished mats are checked for thickness and specification before being stored on site as organised stock, then packed and prepared for dispatch to customers in India and overseas.

Manufacturing Facilities

Where Our Products Take Shape

Dedicated areas for preparation, production and finishing.

Divine Rubber Products

Eara, Neelamperoor, Alleppey District, Kerala

Established in 2002 near Kottayam, India's rubber town, and Alleppey, its coir town, our facility manufactures rubber, coir and jute floor mats for domestic, industrial, agricultural and special purposes.

RSS-4 rubber, hand-searched micro crumb and internationally branded chemicals are prepared before production.

Mats are inspected against their specification, then stored on site and packed for dispatch to customers in India and overseas.
