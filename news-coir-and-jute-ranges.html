Natural Ranges: Coir and Jute from Kerala

16 October 2026 Divine Rubber Team 1 min read

Alongside rubber matting, Divine Rubber offers natural coir and jute products — made in Alleppey District, about an hour from Alleppey, India's coir town.

Coir

Hard, flexible and bio-degradable, coir mats provide excellent scrubbing action. The range includes:

  • Brush mats and coco rubber mats — coir with a rubber backing, hard-wearing and elegant.
  • Coco Rubber Matting and Coir Matting Boucle for larger areas.
  • Woven creal, hollander, rope-weave and fibre mats.
  • Coir fenders.

Jute

Eco-friendly jute loop mats, jute rugs and carpets, hand-spun jute and jute dot mattings bring a natural texture to interiors. The range also includes short-term erosion-control mats.

Choosing between them

Coir works hardest at covered entrances, where its stiff fibres scrub shoes clean. Jute is softer and best in dry indoor rooms. Our guide Rubber, Coir or Jute compares all three materials.
