Coir: Kerala's Natural Fibre at Your Doorstep

25 August 2026 Divine Rubber Team 3 min read

ℹDraft for review — the Divine Rubber team will confirm this content and set a publication date before it goes live.

Divine Rubber is based in Alleppey District — close to Alleppey, India's coir town. Coir, the fibre from coconut husks, has been made into mats here for generations.

Why coir works at the door

Coir mats are hard, flexible and bio-degradable, and they provide excellent scrubbing action — the stiff fibres brush dirt and grit off shoes before it reaches the floor inside.

Choosing a coir mat

  • Brush mats — for maximum scrubbing action at the door.
  • Coco rubber mats — coir with a rubber backing: hard-wearing and elegant.
  • Coir matting boucle — a flexible, hard-wearing woven matting for larger areas.
  • Woven mats — creal, hollander and rope-weave designs.

A natural choice

Because coir is a natural, bio-degradable fibre, it is a good fit for homes and businesses that prefer eco-friendly materials. Browse the coir & jute range.
