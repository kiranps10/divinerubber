Product Spotlight: Interlocking Coin and Tile Mats

16 October 2026 Divine Rubber Team 1 min read

When a large area needs a durable, cushioned rubber floor, interlocking tiles can be laid — and lifted — one tile at a time. Two formats in our range do exactly that.

Interlocking Coin Mat

Large 1 m × 1 m interlocking tiles, 22 mm thick, with a coin design on both sides. Interlocking edges join the tiles into a continuous floor of any size.

Interlocking Tile Mat

Compact 50 cm × 50 cm tiles, 20 mm thick, that are easy to handle and fit around corners and obstacles.

At a glance

  • Coin Mat: 1 m × 1 m · 22 mm · coin design both sides · black
  • Tile Mat: 50 cm × 50 cm · 20 mm · interlocking edges · black

Where they fit

Both formats suit stables and cattle sheds, gyms and fitness areas, and industrial floors. Larger tiles cover open floors quickly; smaller tiles make it easier to work around posts, equipment and corners. For stalls, see also our interlocking stable mats.

Read more in Rubber Matting for Factories, Warehouses and Workshops, or ask us about the best format for your floor.
