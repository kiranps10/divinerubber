How to Clean and Maintain Rubber, Coir and Jute Mats

21 July 2026 Divine Rubber Team 3 min read

ℹDraft for review — the Divine Rubber team will confirm this content and set a publication date before it goes live.

A clean mat works better and lasts longer. These general routines suit most rubber, coir and jute mats.

Rubber mats

  • Shake or sweep off loose dirt regularly.
  • Lift open-hole mats and sweep or hose the floor underneath.
  • Wash with water and a mild detergent, rinse and let the mat dry.
  • Lay mats flat so edges don't curl.

Coir mats

  • Shake or beat the mat outdoors to release grit from the fibres.
  • Vacuum or brush regularly.
  • Let wet mats dry fully before using them again.

Jute mats and rugs

  • Vacuum regularly and keep jute dry.
  • Blot spills straight away rather than rubbing them.
  • Use jute indoors; pair it with a rubber or coir mat at wet entrances.
