Slip Resistance: Choosing Mats for Wet and Polished Floors

16 October 2026 Divine Rubber Team 1 min read

Slips happen where floors are wet, greasy or highly polished. The right mat gives feet something to grip and keeps liquid off the walking surface.

Choose a textured surface

Texture is the first line of defence. The Octagonal Pattern mat has a surface finish that does not slip even on highly polished floors. Diamond-top surfaces — on the Diamond Top Runner and the diamond-top stable mat — and coin surfaces also give confident footing.

Keep liquid moving

Standing water makes any surface slippery. Open-hole hollow mats let water drain through, so the surface people walk on stays drier — useful in kitchens, washrooms and outdoor entrances. Read more in our drainage mats guide.

Place mats where the risk is

  • Entrances — where rain and mud come in. Use a scraper mat outside and a drying mat inside.
  • Kitchens and food counters — where water and grease reach the floor.
  • Washrooms — the Urinal Mat is shaped for urinals and helps keep floors clean.
  • Steps and ramps — the narrow Step Mat (25 × 75 cm) fits stair treads.
  • Corridors — a runner cut to length covers the whole walking line.

Make the mat itself safe

  • Weight: heavier rubber mats stay flat and are less likely to shift.
  • Edges: a taper edge gives a sloped transition from floor to mat.
  • Size: a mat should be big enough that people step on it with both feet, not half on, half off.

Keep mats clean and flat

A dirty or curled mat can become a hazard itself. Regular cleaning keeps the texture working — see our mat care guide.
