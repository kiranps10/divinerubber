Slip Resistance: Choosing Mats for Wet and Polished Floors

11 August 2026 Divine Rubber Team 3 min read

ℹDraft for review — the Divine Rubber team will confirm this content and set a publication date before it goes live.

Slips happen where floors are wet, greasy or highly polished. The right mat gives feet something to grip and keeps liquid off the walking surface.

Choose a textured surface

Texture is the first line of defence. The Octagonal Pattern mat has a surface finish that does not slip even on highly polished floors, and diamond-top and coin surfaces also give confident footing.

Keep liquid moving

Standing water makes any surface slippery. Open-hole hollow mats let water drain through, so the surface people walk on stays drier — useful in kitchens, washrooms and outdoor entrances.

Place mats where the risk is

  • Entrances, where rain and mud come in
  • Kitchens and food counters
  • Washrooms — for example with a urinal mat
  • Steps and ramps — a narrow step mat fits treads

Keep mats clean and flat

A dirty or curled mat can become a hazard itself. Heavier rubber mats stay flat, and regular cleaning keeps the texture working.
