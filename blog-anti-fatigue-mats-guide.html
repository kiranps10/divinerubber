Anti-Fatigue Mats: Comfort for People Who Stand at Work

16 October 2026 Divine Rubber Team 1 min read

Standing on a hard floor for a whole shift is tiring. An anti-fatigue mat puts a cushioned layer between feet and floor, so people can work more comfortably.

How they work

The Anti-Fatigue Bubble Mat has a raised bubble surface. The rubber bubbles create a cushioned, massaging surface that helps reduce strain on feet, legs and back during long shifts. Stud mats work in a similar way: flexible rubber studs give an acupressure-like cushioning effect.

Where they help most

  • Workstations and assembly benches
  • Shop, bar and reception counters
  • Production lines and packing areas
  • Kitchens and food-preparation areas

Choosing the right design

  • Dry standing positions: the Anti-Fatigue Bubble Mat, well suited to workstations, counters and production lines.
  • Hotels and commercial areas: the Rubber Anti-Fatigue Mat measures 1530 × 910 mm and is 14 mm thick.
  • Doorways and smaller spots: a 1 cm Taper Edged Stud Mat, in six sizes from 40 × 70 cm to 80 × 140 cm.
  • Wet areas: an open-hole hollow mat, so liquid drains away from the standing surface.

Placing anti-fatigue mats

  • Cover the whole area where a person stands and moves, not just one spot.
  • Make sure edges lie flat so they do not become a trip hazard.
  • Keep walkways clear — mats belong at standing positions.

Keep it clean

Rubber anti-fatigue mats are washable — lift, sweep or wash them regularly so the surface stays comfortable and safe. Our mat care guide has simple routines.
