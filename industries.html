Industries Overview

Matting for Domestic, Industrial, Agricultural & Special Purposes

Our mats are used in a wide range of environments — from factories, commercial kitchens and hospitality spaces to retail areas, agricultural facilities, residential spaces and public locations. Durable, functional matting that supports safety, cleanliness and comfort in everyday use.

Our range includes solutions for busy entrances, commercial kitchens, workplaces, retail spaces and outdoor areas. Depending on the application, different mat designs can help manage dirt, improve underfoot grip, support drainage and protect floor surfaces. Choosing the right mat depends on the environment, foot traffic and specific usage requirements.
