What Makes a Good Rubber Mat? Five Things to Check

16 October 2026 Divine Rubber Team 2 min read

Two mats can look alike in a photo and perform very differently on the floor. These five checks help you tell them apart before you order.

1. What is it made of?

Ask about the raw materials. Our mats use RSS-4 rubber, hand-searched micro crumb and internationally branded chemicals — materials chosen for mats that are durable, flexible, washable and weatherproof. A mat made from poor compound can crack, harden or lose its shape early.

2. Is it thick enough for the job?

Thickness should match the traffic and the load:

  • Home doorways: a 1 cm stud mat is usually enough.
  • Busy entrances and work areas: heavier hollow mats in 16 mm or 22 mm.
  • Livestock: 17–18 mm stable mats, such as the 4 ft × 6 ft Diamond Top Stable Mat.
  • Large heavy-duty floors: 20–22 mm interlocking tiles.

3. Does the surface grip?

A textured surface keeps feet — and hooves — steady. The Octagonal Pattern mat has a surface finish that does not slip even on highly polished floors, and diamond-top and coin surfaces also give confident footing. Read more in our slip resistance guide.

4. Where does the water go?

In wet areas, water should drain through or away from the mat. Open-hole hollow mats let water and dirt fall through; on the Universal Pattern, a studded bottom helps drainage too.

5. Is it easy to clean?

Good mats trap dirt but release it when cleaned. Rubber mats can be lifted, shaken, swept or hosed down. See our cleaning and maintenance guide.

Before you order

  • Confirm the size, thickness and edge type you need.
  • Check the colour options — some mats come in more than one colour.
  • Ask about quantities and delivery for your location.
Need specifications for a particular mat? Ask our team for sizes, thicknesses and colours.
