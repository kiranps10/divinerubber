Who We Are

A Trusted Name in Rubber, Coir & Jute Matting

Divine Rubber Products was established in 2002 at Eara, Neelamperoor, in the Alleppey District of Kerala, South India — close to Kottayam, India's rubber town, and Alleppey, its coir town.

We manufacture and export high-quality rubber, coir and jute floor mats for domestic, industrial, agricultural and special purposes. RSS-4 rubber, hand-searched high-quality micro crumb and other internationally branded chemicals are used as raw materials for our mats. We supply both the trade and end users, and can supply almost every type of rubber matting.

Manufacturing Excellence

How We Make a Difference

Up-to-date technology and carefully selected natural materials go into every mat, from raw rubber to final dispatch.

Explore Our Manufacturing Process

  1. Raw Materials

    RSS-4 rubber, hand-searched micro crumb and branded chemicals.

  2. Mixing

    Rubber compounds are blended on mixing mills.

  3. Moulding

    Compounds are moulded and cured into the mat pattern.

  4. Finishing

    Edges are trimmed and mats sized to specification.

  5. Inspection

    Finished mats are checked before approval.

  6. Dispatch

    Packed and shipped to trade and export customers.
