Built for Everyday Use

Practical design.
Reliable performance.

Diamond Top Runner comes with custom colour and logo choices to give your area a sleek and personalised feel.

Key features

    Washable surfaces that trap dust and dirt very effectively.

  • Weatherproof

    Suitable for exterior entrances and wet areas.
