Built for Everyday Use

Practical design.
Reliable performance.

Stud and pin mats are covered with hundreds of flexible rubber studs, bristles or fingers that give an acupressure-like cushioning effect and are better at removing mud.

The diamond-pattern centre panel adds a decorative touch for entrances and walkways.

Key features

  • Cushioning Effect

    Flexible rubber studs give an acupressure-like cushioning effect.

  • Easy to Clean

    Washable surfaces that trap dust and dirt very effectively.

  • Durable

    Tough, long-lasting rubber made with RSS-4 rubber and branded chemicals.

  • Weatherproof

    Suitable for exterior entrances and wet areas.
