Drainage Mats: Keeping Wet Areas Safe and Clean

16 October 2026 Divine Rubber Team 1 min read

In wet areas, a solid mat can trap water on top. Drainage mats are designed to let water — and dirt — pass straight through, keeping the walking surface cleaner and drier.

How drainage mats work

Hollow rubber mats have an open-hole top. Water and dirt fall through the holes instead of collecting on the surface. On the Universal Pattern mat, a studded bottom enhances drainage and cleanliness and adds comfort underfoot.

Where to use them

  • Restaurants and kitchens — the Restaurant Mat (3 ft × 3 ft) covers large areas without connectors.
  • Washrooms — the Urinal Mat is shaped for urinals and helps keep floors clean.
  • Entrances and walkways — hollow mats wipe shoes while rain drains away.
  • Playgrounds and outdoor areas — open-hole matting suits wet, exposed spaces.

Covering larger areas

Universal Pattern mats are modular: connectors join them to cover larger floor areas. Sizes run from 60 × 40 cm up to 150 × 100 cm, in 16 mm and 22 mm thickness and in black, terracotta and green. A taper-edged version gives a sloped edge where people walk on and off.

Laying drainage mats

  • Lay on a firm, level surface so water can run away underneath.
  • Where possible, place mats over or towards a floor drain.
  • Leave access for lifting mats to clean the floor below.

Cleaning drainage mats

Because dirt falls through, drainage mats are easy to maintain: lift the mat, sweep or hose the floor underneath and rinse the mat itself before putting it back.
